## 0. 使用顺序（别跳步）

1. 先出 **P1 正立面**（最能暴露造型问题）→ 造型确认后再出其他视角。
2. 再用 **P1 的成品图作为 image1 参考**出 **P2 70° 俯视**（保证同一栋楼）。
3. P2 通过后，用同一底图出 **P3 夜景**（只改光，不改结构）。
4. 最后出 **P4 远景剪影**（做缩略图识别测试）。

> 直接跳过 P1 去出俯视图，十有八九会出现"正面一栋、俯视另一栋"的两栋楼。

## 1. 槽位表

| 槽位 | 可选值 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| `{{VIEW}}` | `2D top-down RPG view, ground seen from directly above, only the front facade visible, roof as a trapezoid above the wall, no side walls` / `full-width elevation study, flat and straight-on, for design confirmation only` / `side elevation` / `street scene from a distance` | 机位。**游戏资产统一用第一种** |
| `{{TIME}}` | `flat ambient daylight` / `warm dusk, firelight from windows` / `night, lantern lit` | 时段（结构不变，只改光） |
| `{{SEASON}}` | `late summer` / `autumn` / `winter with snow on the roof` | 季节只换墙脚草与瓦上雪，木瓦石 9 色不动 |
| `{{DISTANCE}}` | `full frame` / `silhouette at 10 percent scale` | 远景测试用 |

### 1.1 视角关键词：禁用 / 推荐（这一节最容易踩坑）

| 禁用词（会画出侧墙+透视） | 推荐词（才是参考图的角度） |
|---|---|
| `three-quarter view` / `3/4 view` | `2D top-down RPG view` |
| `isometric` / `isometric tilt` | `flat orthographic projection` |
| `perspective view` / `vanishing point` | `front facade only, drawn flat` |
| `front elevation`（这是纯立面，没有屋顶面） | `vertical left and right edges, parallel` |
| `angled view` / `corner view` | `no side walls, no left side, no right side` |

**投影三句话（写进每条 prompt，别省）**：
1. 地面**从正上方**看（草坪/道路/物件都要有俯视感）；
2. 建筑**只露正面墙**，左右边缘垂直、无侧墙、无透视收敛；
3. 屋顶**从上方**看成一个梯形，**占的竖向比前墙更高**，檐口比屋脊宽、两侧出挑。

**比例硬指标（不写就会出成"正面视图"）**：
- 建筑只占画面宽度 **≈1/3**，**四周都留草地**（左/右/下都要能看到地面），不要顶满画幅；
- 屋顶占建筑总高 **55–60%**，前墙只占 **40–45%**（参考图就是这么分的：屋顶明显压过墙体）；
- 屋面瓦垄从檐口**向屋脊方向后退**（coursing recedes toward the ridge），脊线明显短于檐口线。

## 5. 生成参数与后处理

| 项 | 值 |
|---|---|
| size | `1536x1024`（横构图；P1 立面若有透视压缩可改 `1024x1536` 试一版） |
| quality | `high` |
| 参考图 | P2/P3/P4 都传 `image1` = P1 成品，`input_fidelity=high` |
| 裁切 | 先裁掉画框与水印；水印若压在墙体/石台上，**不要裁**，用同行左右纹理复制修补 + 12px 接缝羽化 |
| 降采样 | 只在**整数比**下用 NEAREST；非整数比先重采样到整数倍再降 |
| 目标尺寸 | 地图 sprite `320×224`（10×7 格 @1x）+ `@2x` `640×448` |
| 校验 1 | 色数：整图 ≤ 色板可用色数，且不出现色板外的"新色"超过 3 个 |
| 校验 2 | 灰度可辨阶：把成品转灰度，屋顶 / 墙 / 地面三者的中位亮度差 ≥ 12 |
| 校验 3 | 缩略图测试：缩到 10% 仍能靠"陡坡顶 + 烟囱 + 悬挑招牌 + 披屋"认出酒馆 |
| 校验 4 | 数点：正面 4 窗（含二层 3）、1 主门、背面 1 后门 + 2 高窗、1 烟囱、1 招牌 |

## 6. 出图工作流（固定三段循环）

> 本项目不再使用"降级方案 / 退路"这类分支。统一走一条路：**出图 → 程序化 + 人工修 → 再 AI 生成**。

| 段 | 做什么 | 产出 | 判据 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① 出图 | 用 §8.3 的 prompt 直接生成 1280×768 | raw 图 | 只看构图与竖向比例，不看细节 |
| ② 程序化 + 人工修 | 裁框 / 去水印 / 整数倍降采样；桶顶、窗数、门、接缝这类**局部结构**用像素手术；明显画错处人工补 | 1x 底图 | 数点通过、无补丁痕 |
| ③ 再 AI 生成 | 以②的成品作 `image1`、`input_fidelity=high`，prompt 只写"重绘成一张干净的图，布局与物件数不变" | 洁净成品 | 补丁痕消失、布局与②一致 |

> 这条流程在本项目已反复验证：**手改/程序修过的版本再交给 AI 重绘，补丁痕迹会被全部化掉，且布局不漂**。反过来（直接反复重掷）只会换来一套新画风。

## 8. v2：双层结构强化版（仅提示词，未生成）

> 用户要求：以 `tavern_ext_05_topdown_final.png` 的俯视角度为基础，**突出双层结构**。
> 上一版的问题：屋顶占了建筑高度的近一半，墙面只剩一条窄带，只能排下一排窗 → 看上去像单层小屋。

### 8.1 三层竖向配比（把数字写死，别让 AI 自由发挥）

| 部位 | 上一版（问题） | v2 目标 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 屋顶 | ≈ 50–58% | **42–45%** | 仍要读得出坡面与瓦垄，但不能压死墙面 |
| 二层 | 无（被压没了） | **24–26%** | 一排 3 扇窗 + 花箱 |
| 一层 | 与二层合并成一条带 ≈ 42% | **26–28%** | 拱门 + 左右各 1 扇窗 |
| 合计 | 屋顶过大 | **立面（一+二层）≈ 55%，屋顶 ≈ 45%** | 立面必须比屋顶高 |

### 8.2 让"二层"读得出来的四个信号（缺一个就会糊成单层）

1. **两排窗**：一层 2 窗（带百叶）+ 二层 3 窗（带花箱），排数必须一眼可数。
2. **一条贯穿的腰线**：二层整体向外出挑 1/4 格，腰线下沿有 1px 投影 —— 这是分层的第一信号。
3. **窗型差异**：一层窗大、二层窗略小且靠近檐口，靠尺寸差再强调一次。
4. **檐下窄带**：屋顶与二层窗之间留一条 3–4px 的木/灰泥带（阁楼带），别让窗贴到檐口。

### 8.3 完整 prompt（v2.1 · 修正视角）

> v2 的坑：只写了"只露正面墙"，没压画幅比例 → AI 把建筑画到 ~55% 宽、四周几乎没草地，又读成正视图。
> **结论：俯视感来自"地面占满画幅"，不是来自"屋顶画出来"。**

```
A 2D top-down RPG scene. A wide flat lawn, seen from DIRECTLY ABOVE, fills the entire frame.
A dirt path enters at the bottom edge of the image and leads up to the building.

CAMERA: the camera looks down at about 55 to 60 degrees. The GROUND is seen from directly above -
grass tufts, small stones, a dirt path, a low wooden fence and a stone well are all drawn as seen
from above. The BUILDING shows only its front facade, drawn flat with vertical left and right edges,
no side walls, no left side, no right side, no perspective, no vanishing point. Its roof is seen
from above as a FORESHORTENED TRAPEZOID whose eave line is clearly wider than its ridge line, and the
roof surface is visible as a plane receding upward.

FRAMING (strict): the building occupies about 45 percent of the frame width and stands in the middle
of the lawn. At least 12 percent of the frame width is empty lawn on the left AND on the right, and
at least 12 percent of the frame height is empty lawn below the building. The building must NOT fill
the frame. No horizon line, no sky.

BUILDING SHAPE: the building is WIDE. Its front facade is clearly WIDER than it is tall and spans
FIVE EQUAL BAYS (five vertical bays separated by timber posts). It reads as a low, wide village inn,
NOT as a tall narrow tower.

VERTICAL PROPORTIONS of the building:
- ground storey 27 percent
- upper storey 25 percent
- attic band 3 percent
- roof 45 percent
The front facade is therefore TALLER than the roof is deep.

THE BUILDING IS CLEARLY TWO STOREYS, obvious at a glance from two horizontal rows of windows and one
strong continuous line between them.

GROUND STOREY: rough stone plinth of two courses, dark half-timber frame with pale plaster panels;
one SMALL arched stone doorway exactly in the centre - the door opening is only about ONE FIFTH of
the facade width and only about two thirds of the ground storey height, a modest everyday doorway,
NOT a grand entrance and NOT wider than one bay; a plank double door with four horizontal iron
straps, two stone steps, a worn threshold; TWO windows with half-open wooden shutters on EACH side
of the door.

FLOOR DIVISION: the upper storey overhangs by a quarter tile; a continuous horizontal timber line
runs across the whole facade with exposed joist ends and a 1px dark shadow under it, edge to edge.

UPPER STOREY: EXACTLY FOUR windows with cross mullions, slightly smaller than the ground-floor
windows, each with a herb flower box, evenly spaced across the wide facade and clearly above the
division line.

ATTIC BAND: a narrow strip of plaster and timber between the upper windows and the eaves.

ROOF: the ridge is a HORIZONTAL line running left-to-right across the image, parallel to the front
wall - NEVER a vertical ridge and never a gable end facing the viewer. The near roof plane between
the ridge and the eave is ONE single large trapezoid, wider at the eave than at the ridge, seen from
above. Steep gable roof, terracotta tiles in visible courses, a lighter
ridge cap, deep eaves with exposed rafter tails, one square stone chimney left of the ridge centre,
moss along the ridge, two missing tiles, one small gabled dormer on the near slope.

SIGNAGE: a wrought-iron bracket above and to the right of the door carrying a double-sided wooden
board showing only a cup and a bowl, no text; an iron wall lantern left of the door.

AROUND THE BUILDING: two wooden barrels, one crate and one long bench against the plinth; a low
wooden fence at the right; a stone well at the left.

STYLE: pixel art, crisp hard pixel edges, one closed 1px dark outline around every object, flat
3-4 tone shading, no gradients, no dithering, no anti-aliasing, no blur.
PALETTE: wood #503818 #785028 #906838, roof #6A3024 #884030 #A85A40, stone #585848 #787868 #A09888,
plaster #C4BEB0, iron #7A8290 #C9CED6, moss #495A37, grass #5E6E43 #909952, fire #E8963C #F6D08A.

Flat even ambient daylight, no window light beams, no bright patches, no large cast shadows.
No characters, no text, no watermark, no lettering.
```

### 8.4 验收（数点，别靠肉眼感觉）

| # | 检查 | 通过标准 |
|---|---|---|
